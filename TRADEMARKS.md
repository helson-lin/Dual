## You may | 允许

- Refer to the project by name to describe it truthfully, e.g. "based on Dual" or "a fork of Dual".
- Use the unmodified name and icon when linking to or writing about the official project.
- Redistribute unmodified official release builds as published by this repository.

- 为如实描述而提及项目名称，例如“基于 Dual”“Dual 的分支”。
- 在链接或介绍官方项目时使用未经修改的名称和图标。
- 原样再分发本仓库发布的官方构建。

## You may not, without prior written permission | 未经事先书面许可，不得

- Distribute a modified version, fork, or derivative app under the name "Dual" or a
  confusingly similar name, or with the Dual icon or a confusingly similar icon.
- Use the Marks in a way that suggests endorsement by, or affiliation with, the project
  or its author.
- Use the Marks as part of another product, company, domain, or app store listing name.

- 以“Dual”或易混淆的名称、或以 Dual 图标或易混淆的图标，发布修改版、分支或衍生应用。

## Forks | 分支

If you distribute a modified version, you must rename it and replace the app icon
(including `Dual/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset`) and change the bundle identifier.
Your fork remains fully usable under GPLv3 otherwise.

发布修改版时，必须更换名称、替换应用图标（包括 `Dual/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset`）
并修改 Bundle ID。除此之外，你的分支在 GPLv3 下的权利不受影响。

For permission requests, please open an issue at https://github.com/helson-lin/Dual/issues.
